## Brand & Style
The design system articulates an elevated creator-studio ecosystem tailored for high-output social media strategists, brands, and operators. Merging structural geometric precision with luminous digital energy, the visual language balances effortless executive efficiency with vibrant creator momentum.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Minimalism** with **Sculptural Tactility**:
- Crisp, clinical light surfaces grounded in pale zinc and cool atmospheric whites.
- Vibrant, high-chroma electric indigo driving focus, accented by channel-tuned chromatic sparks (cyan and magenta).
- Pilliform containment and sculpted radii that soften functional data density into an approachable, high-touch luxury workflow.
- High-legibility typographic tension between dynamic geometric display headings and balanced, ergonomic body metrics.

## Colors
The palette leverages a crisp, luminous foundation with focused chromatic energy to demarcate multi-channel performance and contextual status:

- **Primary (`#6366F1`)**: Electric Indigo. Serves as the primary operational anchor, designated for high-intent actions, key activation states, active navigation indicators, and primary visual focal points.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4`)**: Electric Cyan. Applied to dynamic delivery metrics, incoming velocity charts, secondary content tagging, and rapid feed diagnostics.
- **Tertiary (`#EC4899`)**: Electric Rose. Deployed for high-engagement metrics, audience expansion triggers, alert badges, and campaign conversion indicators.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate Neutral. Orchestrates functional UI architecture. The scale spans from pure white `#FFFFFF` through canvas foundations (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`) up to deep charcoal slate (`#0F172A`) for high-contrast typographic rendering.

### Functional Roles & Contrast
- **Canvas Base**: `#F8FAFC` provides cool ambient separation from card surfaces.
- **Surface Elevation**: Pure `#FFFFFF` preserves structural distinction across elevated panels.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Low-contrast hairpins (`#E2E8F0` at 1px) ensure clean grid alignment without visual weight.
- **Platform Accents**: Dedicated semantic tokens map directly to platform channel identities while preserving core luminance.

## Typography
The typographic architecture pairs the sculptural, geometric flair of **Outfit** for headlines and impact analytics with the neutral, hyper-legible ergonomics of **Manrope** for analytical dashboards and operational text.

- **Display & Headlines (`Outfit`)**: Tight tracking, strong geometric presence, and proportional balance establish clarity across dense dashboards and feed management cards.
- **Metrics (`Outfit 700`)**: Key social metrics (reach, impressions, growth velocity) utilize numeric weights optimized for immediate visual scanning.
- **Body & Controls (`Manrope`)**: Optimized vertical metrics and balanced apertures ensure comfortable reading of complex copy schedules, message threads, and configuration panels.
- **Labels (`Manrope 600/700`)**: Compact, slightly tracked labels provide crisp taxonomy for platform identifiers, filters, and status badges.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a mobile-first fluid grid structured around an **8pt spatial rhythm** (with 4pt micro-steps for tight badge and metric pairings).

- **Mobile Viewport (360px – 480px)**: 4-column layout, `1rem` outer screen margin, and `0.75rem` gutters. Maximum vertical density to keep composition targets and quick-schedule inputs within thumb reach.
- **Tablet / Expanded Viewport (481px – 840px)**: 8-column layout, `1.5rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Metric streams reflow from single-column vertical ribbons to a 2x2 multi-card layout.
- **Desktop / Studio Viewport (841px+)**: 12-column layout maxing out at `1280px` centered canvas, `2rem` margins, enabling an anchor sidebar, unified multi-stream feed, and real-time inspector rail.

Content maintains clear vertical spacing: cards feature `space-lg` internal padding, while compact badge clusters use `space-xs` and `space-sm` offsets.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is expressed through **tinted atmospheric luminosity** paired with delicate, high-precision surface borders rather than heavy opacity drop-shadows.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F8FAFC`, non-elevated flat plane for app backgrounds and inactive tab bars.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a subtle tinted shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.04), 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` and a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Active Tabs & Modals)**: `#FFFFFF` with `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.06)`, framed by a 1px border of `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Command Surfaces & Toasts)**: High-blur translucent or solid white pilliform panels (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes
The design system embraces a **Pilliform Geometric** shape vocabulary:
- **Base Components**: Inputs, pill badges, channel tags, primary triggers, and floating selectors utilize `9999px` full-radius contours (`rounded-full` / pill-shaped).
- **Enclosures & Cards**: Dashboard metric containers, post previews, and dialog surfaces adopt a softened `rounded-lg` (2rem) or `rounded-xl` (3rem) boundary, establishing visual continuity with the curved pill badges.
- **Media Thumbnails**: Visual assets and avatar clusters balance the pill aesthetic with proportional `1rem` soft rounding to maintain image crop integrity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Full pill contour (`rounded-full`), solid `#6366F1` background, `#FFFFFF` text (`label-lg`), subtle interactive shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.28)`. Height: 44px mobile, 48px desktop.
- **Secondary**: Pill contour, 1.5px border of `#6366F1`, transparent background, `#6366F1` text. 
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Pill contour, transparent fill, `#64748B` text, shifting to `#0F172A` with `#F1F5F9` background fill on press/hover.

### Platform Badges & Chips
- **Geometry**: Continuous pill shape with 6px vertical padding, 12px horizontal padding.
- **Variants**:
  - *Instagram*: Subtle magenta wash (`#FDF2F8`), `#EC4899` text and icon dot.
  - *TikTok / Cyan Channel*: Subtle cyan wash (`#ECFEFF`), `#0891B2` text.
  - *X / Platform Generic*: Subtle slate wash (`#F1F5F9`), `#334155` text.
  - *LinkedIn / High Focus*: Subtle indigo wash (`#EEF2FF`), `#4F46E5` text.
- **Filter Chips**: Interactive toggle pills; selected states switch to solid neutral `#0F172A` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Metric Cards
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`, `rounded-lg` (2rem), 1px border `#E2E8F0`, interior padding `space-lg`.
- Top row: Contextual platform chip paired with time-window selector.
- Center: Typographic metric (`Outfit 700`, 30px) in `#0F172A`.
- Delta: Trend indicator pill (`+18.4%`) with muted green or electric secondary background.

### Input Fields
- Enclosed within a full pill silhouette (`rounded-full`), height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#CBD5E1`.
- Interior padding `1rem 1.25rem`. Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`. Active focus triggers a 2px `#6366F1` outline ring with `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)` halo.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes**: 20px box with `0.5rem` rounded corners. Checked state: `#6366F1` fill with sharp white check vector.
- **Radios**: 20px full circle, checked state shows a centered 8px `#6366F1` solid pip inside a `#FFFFFF` core bordered by `#6366F1`.

### Bottom Navigation & Tab Bars
- Floating pill dock suspended `1rem` above viewport base.
- Frosted translucent finish (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- Active item marked by a solid electric indigo micro-pill indicator and primary icon tint. Inactive icons remain in `#64748B`.
